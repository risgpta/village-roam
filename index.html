Everything here is marked on the map (Tab), and signposts at the crossroads point the way. Roads link every village; buses and jeeps run between them.

Gaon and close by

Gaonगाँव

The home village: the chaupal square under the old banyan, the temple and its bell, the well, the chai stall, the haat (market), the school, the cricket ground, mud houses with Warli paintings.

The melaमेला

The village fair, out past the edge of the village: a giant wheel, a merry-go-round, swings and stalls of sweets and bangles. At night its lights come on.

The ghat and the riverघाट

Stone steps down to the water, the rowing boat, the footbridge, buffaloes wallowing below the ghat.

Paar Gaonपार गाँव

The little hamlet across the river, over the footbridge.

Chhota Gaonछोटा गाँव

A small village up the road, with the post office and the hospital.

The charagahचारागाह

The grazing meadow, where the shepherds bring their sheep, goats and cows.

Other villages

Marudharमारुधर

A Rajasthani village of sandstone and blue houses round a stepwell, over the road bridge.

Banglagramबांग्लाग्राम

A Bengali village of curved-roof huts round a pond.

Thennoorतेन्नूर

A Tamil village with tiled roofs, verandahs and a gopuram temple.

Vitthalwadiविट्ठलवाड़ी

A Maharashtrian village of basalt and tile, with its wada and Vitthal temple.

Pahadi Gaonपहाड़ी गाँव

A hill village of stone and slate, with apple trees.

Bamboo Hillबाँस पहाड़ी

Bamboo stilt houses of the North-East and a terraced tea garden.

Noorpur and Mariapurनूरपुर · मरियापुर

A Muslim village round its masjid, and a Christian village round its church — both by the railway.

Khetpura and Rannvasखेतपुरा · रणवास

By the ring railway: Punjabi farmers in Khetpura; Kutchi herders, goats and a camel in Rannvas.

Dhaniyapur, Nadipar, Pathargaonधनियापुर · नदीपार · पत्थरगाँव

The far villages: Bihari mud houses, Bengali huts by the little river, a stone hill village.

Out in the country

The hilltop shrine and Shikharशिखर

The stone stairway climbs the great mountain to the summit temple. The orange shrine shuttle bus runs up the hill road from the village and back.

Jharna — the waterfallझरना

A tall waterfall far out in the hills, its stream running down a gorge to the river.

The airstripहवाई पट्टी

Out in the open country: the plane, the helicopter and the hangar.

Sonpur station and the railwayसोनपुर

Trains run between the stations; the ring line circles the whole land, through tunnels and over the viaduct across the Ghati valley.

The wildजंगल

Beyond the ring: the jungle with lions, hyenas, monkeys and snakes; rhinos in the Gainda hills; tigers, leopards, wolves and elephants in the far hills; crocodiles on the far river banks. Keep your distance!
